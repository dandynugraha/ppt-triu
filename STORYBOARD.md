# STORYBOARD.md — one continuous 8 s shot (192 frames @ 24 fps)

**Override in force (Dandy, this run):** no image/video generation API or website. Phases 5.4–5.5 are replaced by a procedural render: `assets/render/scene.html` (WebGL fragment shader + Canvas 2D, seeded RNG, no clocks) rendered by `tools/render.mjs` through Playwright to `deck/frames/f_0001…0192.webp`. Illustrative only. Nothing resembles an MRI slice, a protein render, a chart or microscopy: it is a glossy abstract orb, line-drawn branching filaments and a smooth curved wall with rounded stalks.

- **Camera:** continuous push-in through three nested scales, constant feel (log-zoom), no cuts.
- **Light:** soft key from upper-left (`L = (-.45,-.55,.7)`), rim light lavender, low specular.
- **Palette (DESIGN.md):** paper #F8F9FB, lavender #EFEEF8, violet-deep #2E2DB8, violet #3D3CD3, lilac #8385ED, mist #C9BEF5, pink #F099C2. Lime is **not** used in the video (reserved for UI emphasis).
- **Texture:** deterministic ±1.1/255 dither in the shader (anti-banding) + 4 % CSS grain overlay in the deck.
- **Must stay empty:** x < 40 % is pure paper in every frame; 40–58.5 % is a paper fade (scrim baked into the frame, plus a CSS scrim in the deck). Text column is x 112–936 px (5.8–48.8 %).
